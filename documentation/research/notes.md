Unordered/unfiltered ideas from brainstorming and the likes will go here:

Requirements from our software:
1. to consider the speakers as non-cooperative (they do not specifically wish to be recognized)
2. to be text-independent (more challenging but better suited for forensics, mainly because of phonetic variability and session variability)
3. to be able to detect deepfakes/synthesized speech/replays or otherwise manipulated audio
4. to be able to transcript the words from a speaker, preferably in real time, and detect the language used automatically

Requirements 1-3 will be implemented by us, during this semester.
For requirement 4, we will use a pre-trained, modern ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) model (like Whisper from OpenAI)
